Frequently asked questions

I'm brand new to Bhangra. Can I join?

Yes — most of our students started with zero experience. Beginner classes assume nothing, and your first class is free so you can see for yourself before paying anything.

When do classes run?

See the weekly schedule — pick your city to see the days. We confirm your exact day and time within one business day of your trial request.

How much do classes cost?

Tuition is month-to-month with a sibling discount. See tuition — [prices to be added].

How do I pay?

Venmo, Zelle, check, or card. Monthly tuition is due at the start of each month.

What's the difference between Bhangra and Giddha?

Bhangra is the high-energy Punjabi folk dance traditionally danced by men and now danced by everyone; Giddha is the traditional women's folk dance, with its own rhythms, boliyan and movement vocabulary. We teach both — Giddha runs in Livermore for adults 18+.

How many levels are there?

Three — beginner, intermediate and advanced — across kids (4–12), teens (13–19) and adults (20+). We place every new student after their trial class.

How do I join the competitive team?

The competitive team is by audition. Choose "Competitive team audition" on the trial form or call us and we'll tell you when the next tryouts are.
